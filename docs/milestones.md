# Milestones

1. Foundation and offline slice: seed validation, guest onboarding, lessons, grading, notes, vocabulary, and review persistence.
2. Supabase identity and synchronization: account upgrade, schema, RLS, conflict handling, and negative policy tests.
3. Situations and community: dialogue completion, author revisions, voting, comments, snippets, reporting, and blocking.
4. Chats and profiles: requests, group roles, text/snippet messages, membership history, public contributions, and trusted awards.
5. Release hardening: localization, accessibility, responsive checks, integration tests, platform builds, and clean-checkout documentation.

AI, voice, notifications, and analytics remain later milestones.
